Title: Privacy requests: export / correct / delete a user's data
Date: 2026-11-01
Priority: High (before going live with real users)
Area: legal / data

What's the problem?
CLAUDE.md §9 (Data Privacy Act, RA 10173) says users can view/download and correct their data,
and ask for account deletion, handled by the super-admin: keep only the order records needed for
legal/dispute reasons, anonymize the rest. Today there's no tool for that. You'd have to edit
many collections by hand (users, chats, private files, push subscriptions, rider profile...).

Why it matters (what could go wrong):
A buyer emails "delete my account" and the job is done halfway: chat photos or a landmark photo
stay behind, or order records get deleted that you must keep. Both are bad under the DPA.

What I suggest (Phase 8.5 or before Phase 9):
1. Admin desk card "Privacy requests" + a button on a user: "Export this user's data" ->
   a JSON file with their profile, consents, orders (their part), and a list of their files.
2. "Delete / anonymize this user":
   - deletes their private files (landmark photo, chat photos, receipts they uploaded, rider docs)
   - deletes their chats' messages (unless a chat is under a report), push subscriptions, sessions
   - keeps orders/store orders (legal records) but replaces name/phone/address/pin with "Deleted user"
   - blocks the account from logging in again; writes an audit entry
3. A "My account" page for buyers (change username, phone, address, landmark photo) covers
   "correct their data" and saves you work.

Effort and cost: Free. About one work session (+ tests).
Trade-offs / risks: Deleting is permanent. The tool must ask for confirmation and log who did it.
The retention period for order records is yours to decide (put it in the Privacy Notice).
What I need from you:
- Yes/no to building this before deploy
- How long to keep order records after an account is deleted (e.g. 5 years?). It's your/your
  lawyer's call, I won't guess it.
