Title: Replace the tutorial's demo super-admin (admin@demo.com)
Date: 2026-10-29
Priority: High
Area: security

What's the problem?
The tutorial's seed script created a super-admin account: admin@demo.com with the password "demo".
It is in your database right now, and it's the ONLY super-admin account. The email and password are
printed in the public tutorial repo and video, so anyone could guess them.
(I fixed the seed script so new seeds use a random password. Your existing account is unchanged —
changing it means writing to the database, so I didn't touch it.)

Why it matters (what could go wrong):
Once the site is online, anyone could log in as super-admin and control everything.
Today the risk is low because the site isn't deployed yet.

What I suggest:
Before any deploy (and ideally now):
  1. Log in to /admin as admin@demo.com.
  2. Create your real super-admin account with your own email and a long password
     (password manager recommended), and give it the super-admin role.
  3. Log in with the new account, then delete admin@demo.com (or at least change its email and password).
Phase 2 adds the full super-admin hardening (hidden login path, 2FA) from CLAUDE.md §10.5.

Effort and cost (free? needs a paid service?):
Free. 5 minutes.

Trade-offs / risks:
Make sure the new account works before deleting the old one, or you lock yourself out.
