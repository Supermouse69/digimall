Title: Allowing buyers under 18 + an "18+ only" item flag
Date: 2026-10-29
Priority: High
Area: legal / data / flow

What's the problem?
The developer wants minors (under 18) to be able to use DigiMall, with sellers able to mark some items
"18+ only" so minors can't see them. That touches several laws, so it needs a decision before we
build sign-up (Phase 2b). This is not legal advice — please confirm with a lawyer or the NPC if unsure.

Why it matters (what could go wrong):
1. Data Privacy Act (RA 10173): we collect name, phone, home address, map pin, and a landmark photo.
   For minors this is riskier, and NPC guidance generally expects consent from a parent/guardian,
   not the child. Getting this wrong is a privacy violation.
2. Civil Code: a minor can't fully consent to a contract; a purchase by a minor can be annulled
   (cancelled) later, which can hurt sellers who already handed over goods.
3. Safety: riders and sellers would be dealing with (and delivering to) children at home.
4. Payments: the buyer pays with their own GCash. Check GCash's own age rules for full accounts —
   a minor may be using a parent's account anyway.
5. The "18+ item" flag: in the beta, the usual adult-only items (alcohol, tobacco, vapes, adult content)
   are already PROHIBITED (CLAUDE.md §9). So the flag would cover few or no items. And it can only rely
   on the age the user types in — it isn't real age verification (no ID check). A minor can simply
   type a fake birth year.

What I suggest:
Option A (recommended for beta): Minimum age 18.
  - Sign-up checkbox: "I am at least 18 years old." Simple, collects no extra data, lowest legal risk.
  - Keep the prohibited-items list as the protection; no 18+ flag needed yet.
  - Revisit minors after DigiMall is a registered business and you've gotten advice.
Option B: Allow minors (e.g. 15–17) with a parent/guardian.
  - Ask for birthdate (needed to know who is a minor — more personal data).
  - Minors must tick "My parent/guardian agrees" + enter the guardian's name and mobile number.
    (A checkbox can't prove the parent really agreed — stronger options cost money.)
  - Products get an "18+ only" toggle; hidden from minors' accounts and from logged-out visitors,
    enforced on the server.
  - The Terms and Privacy Notice (you write them) must explain how minors' data is handled.
  - More work: ~1–2 extra days, plus ongoing moderation.

Effort and cost (free? needs a paid service?):
Option A: free, no extra work. Option B: free, ~1–2 extra days; real ID-based age checks would need a
paid service.

Trade-offs / risks:
A: fewer users (some teens buy snacks/school items at sari-sari stores). B: more legal and safety
exposure, more data to protect, and the 18+ flag is easy to bypass.

What I need from you:
Pick A or B. If B: the minimum age (e.g. 15?) and what kinds of items you'd mark "18+".
